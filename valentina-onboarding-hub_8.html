🕐 How We Work Together
Schedule & Overlap
You manage your own schedule — as long as deliverables are done and we're in sync, you have full flexibility. That said, for the next three months while Daniela is in Europe, we've agreed on a daily overlap window:

⏰ 8:30 AM – 12:30 PM ET 📍 Monday – Friday 🌍 Daniela is in Paris (CET = ET + 6hrs)

This is when we connect, sync on priorities, and handle anything that needs a real-time decision. Outside of this window, Daniela may send messages that you can pick up when you're back on.
WhatsApp Channel
Daniela will send you messages to a dedicated WhatsApp channel throughout the day — including from Europe. You can mute this channel outside of your working hours. When you start your day, check it first. This is where Daniela will drop urgent flags, quick asks, context on meetings, and anything she needs you to act on. Think of it as her direct line to you.
Daily Briefing Email
You send this to Daniela every morning before 8:30 AM ET. It should cover: today's meetings (name, company, LinkedIn profile, context on who they are), any RSVPs that need a response, anything urgent or pending from the day before, and the top 3 priorities for the day. Keep it short, clear, and scannable — Daniela reads it on her phone.

You will have access to Daniela's email. Manage the inbox — flag urgent messages, sort, draft responses for approval. As you get comfortable with her voice and the relationships, you'll move toward sending independently.
LinkedIn + Sales Navigator

Slack
ROI Labs and Drafted both run on Slack. You'll have access to both workspaces. This is where day-to-day team communication happens — monitor channels, respond on Daniela's behalf where appropriate, and flag anything that needs her attention.
AI Tools
Use AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT) actively — for drafting, research, summarizing, formatting. Speed and quality both matter. If you can use AI to do something faster without losing quality, do it.
